02 — The discovery

So, what is Kalari?

Kalari is a traditional milk-based, naturally fermented food associated with the Jammu region — particularly Udhampur. Firm when it's cold. Molten, stretchy and deeply savoury the moment it meets a hot griddle.

Most of India has never tasted it. That's the whole point.

Why should something this good stay known only to a few people?

03 — The harder problem

We didn't just want to serve Kalari.

Moving a regional food to another city is easy to say and hard to do. Freshness, hygiene, consistency, reliability — a tradition only travels when there's a real system behind it.

So UKCO built the system first: a controlled, modern process designed for a QSR that has to deliver the same quality every single day.

Udhampur gave it its roots.
UKCO is giving it a future.
